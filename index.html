What to wear

Snow-fed water stays cold even in summer. Match the layer to the day and the weather. On the tricky days a swim is likeliest, so immersion protection wins over paddling comfort. (Kayakers: spraydeck on for days 3 & 4.)

ConditionsWear
Easy day · fairShorty + PFD
Easy day · poorThermal base layer + cag
Tricky day · fair4/3 wetsuit (core warmth, free shoulders)
Tricky day · poor4/3 + cag — vent it or carry it; don't cook on the 41km
Semi-dry optionThermal + 3mm neoprene trousers + cag

Before day 3: run a full wet-exit rotation and hard strokes on dry land in whatever you'll wear — check nothing binds the shoulders.
